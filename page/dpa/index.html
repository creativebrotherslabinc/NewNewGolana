1. Data Processing Overview

Golana.online acts as a data controller for the limited data transmitted through server-side proxies and external API calls. We do not act as a data processor for any third party.

2. Local Processing

Most file operations (PDF, image, audio, video) occur locally on the user's device. These files are never transmitted to our servers.

3. Server-Side Proxy and External API Processing

The following tools transmit data through our server-side proxies or direct API calls to third-party services:

- IP Location Lookup: IP addresses are forwarded to ipapi.co. - News Feed: Language and region preferences are forwarded to Google News RSS. Article text may be sent to Google Translate for translation. - Map and Location Search: Search queries and coordinates are forwarded to OpenStreetMap and optionally Yelp. - Google Maps Lookup: Business name queries are forwarded to Google Maps. - Cryptocurrency Prices: Cryptocurrency IDs are sent to CoinGecko API. - URL Shortener: URLs are sent to is.gd.

We do not store, log, or retain any data that passes through these proxies. The data is forwarded in real time and discarded immediately after the response is returned.

4. Database-Backed Tools

Some calculator tools fetch reference data (tax brackets, exchange rates) from our Supabase database. Only the selected country and year are sent. No personal data is transmitted.

5. No Data Storage

We do not store, persist, or retain any user data on our servers. Data stored in the user's browser localStorage or IndexedDB remains on the user's device.

6. Legal Basis

Under GDPR Article 6, the legal basis for processing is legitimate interest (providing the requested tool functionality). No personal data is processed for marketing or profiling purposes.

7. Data Subject Rights

Since we do not store personal data, data subject access, rectification, erasure, and portability requests are effectively satisfied by default. Contact golana.online@gmail.com to exercise any rights.

8. Sub-Processors

The third-party services listed above act as independent data controllers under their own terms. We do not control their data practices. Links to their privacy policies: - ipapi.co: https://ipapi.co/privacy-policy/ - Google News: https://policies.google.com/privacy - Google Translate: https://policies.google.com/privacy - OpenStreetMap: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy - Yelp: https://www.yelp.com/static?p=privacy_policy - Google Maps: https://policies.google.com/privacy - CoinGecko: https://www.coingecko.com/en/privacy - is.gd: https://is.gd/privacy.php

9. Compliance

We comply with GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, PIPEDA, PDPA, and global data protection laws.
