1. No Cookies by Default

Golana.online does not set cookies unless optional services are enabled. Essential browser storage (localStorage, IndexedDB) is used for preferences and tool data, but this is not a cookie.

2. Optional Cookies

If you accept optional cookies, the following service may set cookies or use similar technologies:

- Google AdSense: Sets advertising cookies or uses similar technologies for ad measurement and personalization. See Google's advertising privacy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and manage ad settings at https://ads.google.com/home/adsettings.

This technology is controlled by Google and its partners, not golana.online.

3. Types of Cookies Set by Third Parties

- IDE, NID (Google AdSense): Used for ad personalization and measurement. Duration: up to 2 years. - Google may use additional cookies as described in their cookie policies.

4. Your Choice

You can decline optional cookies in the consent notice or disable cookies in your browser settings. Declining optional cookies does not prevent you from using Go LANA's tools. You can also manage your cookie preferences through your browser's cookie settings.

5. Changes

We may update this policy as our use of third-party services changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
