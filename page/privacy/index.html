Golana.online ("we", "our", "the website") provides free online tools, utilities, games, printables, and calculators accessible worldwide. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how our website operates.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

Go LANA does not require an account and does not ask for names, phone numbers, addresses, payment details, or uploaded files. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

2. Local Processing

Most tools run entirely in your browser. Files you upload (PDF, images, text, audio, video) never leave your device. They are processed locally and deleted when you close the page.

3. Server-Side Proxy Services and External APIs

Some tools contact external services through our server-side proxies or direct API calls as part of their normal function. When you use these tools, the following data is transmitted to third parties:

- IP Location Lookup: Your IP address or a queried IP address is sent to ipapi.co to determine approximate geographic location. - News Feed: Your selected language and region preferences are sent to Google News RSS feeds to retrieve headlines. Article titles and descriptions may be sent to Google Translate for translation into your preferred language. - Map and Location Search: Your search queries and coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap (Overpass, Nominatim, Photon) and optionally Yelp to find nearby places. - Google Maps Lookup: Business name queries are sent to Google Maps to retrieve address, phone, website, and hours. - Cryptocurrency Prices: Cryptocurrency names and IDs are sent to CoinGecko API to retrieve current prices and market chart data. - URL Shortener: URLs you enter are sent to is.gd to generate a shortened link.

These transmissions are necessary to provide the tool's function. We do not store the transmitted data on our servers. The third-party services process the data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Database-Backed Tools

Some calculator tools (mortgage, tax, currency) fetch reference data from our Supabase database, such as tax brackets and exchange rates. These requests send only the selected country and year — no personal data is transmitted or stored.

5. Local Storage and IndexedDB

Some tools and games use localStorage or IndexedDB to save game progress, tool settings, visit counters, and preferences. This data stays only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

6. Cookies and Advertising

Go LANA uses essential browser storage for preferences and local tool data. With your permission, Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies to show personalized or non-personalized ads. Google and its partners may use information such as browser identifiers, approximate location, and browsing activity according to their own policies. You can decline optional cookies, change your browser settings, or manage advertising preferences through Google's advertising settings.

7. Third-Party Services and Links

Google AdSense is an optional service loaded only after you accept optional cookies. Its processing is governed by Google's privacy, cookie, and advertising policies. Some tools link to or retrieve data from external services (ipapi.co, Google News, Google Translate, OpenStreetMap, Yelp, Google Maps, CoinGecko, is.gd). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 13. The website is suitable for children to use, and we do not gather any personal information from them.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the right to: access any personal data we hold about you, rectify inaccurate data, erase your data, restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing. Since we do not collect or store personal data, these rights are effectively satisfied by default. To exercise any rights, contact golana.online@gmail.com.

10. Your Rights Under CCPA

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information is collected, request deletion, opt out of the sale of personal information, and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell personal information and do not collect personal data for commercial purposes. To exercise any rights, contact golana.online@gmail.com.

11. International Compliance

We comply with GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, PIPEDA, PDPA, and global privacy laws. Because we do not collect or store personal data, most regulatory requirements are satisfied by default.

12. Security

Because we do not store personal data on our servers, there is minimal risk of data breaches. The server-side proxy services transmit data in transit but do not persist it.

13. Data Retention

We do not retain any user data on our servers. Data stored in localStorage or IndexedDB on your device persists until you clear your browser data.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as new features are added. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
