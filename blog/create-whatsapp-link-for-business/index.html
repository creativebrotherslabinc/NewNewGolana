How to Create a WhatsApp Link for Your Business | Go LANA

Generate clickable wa.me links with pre-filled messages to make it easy for customers to reach your business on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp is one of the most popular messaging platforms for business communication. A clickable WhatsApp link lets customers start a conversation with you instantly, without saving your number first. This reduces friction and increases the number of inquiries you receive.

A WhatsApp link uses the wa.me format. The basic structure is simple: wa.me followed by your phone number in international format, without plus signs or spaces. For example, wa.me/15551234567 opens a chat with the number 1-555-123-4567.

You can also add a pre-filled message to the link. This is done by appending a question mark and the text parameter. When customers click the link, WhatsApp opens with your message already typed in. They just need to press send. This is useful for guiding customers to provide specific information like their name, order number, or question.

To create a WhatsApp link for your business, use a WhatsApp Link Generator tool. Enter your phone number with the country code, type the pre-filled message you want customers to see, and the tool generates the link instantly. You can copy and paste it anywhere.

Where should you place your WhatsApp link? The most effective places are your website header, contact page, email signature, social media bios, and promotional materials. Some businesses add it to their Google Business profile so customers can message directly from search results.

For businesses that receive many inquiries, a WhatsApp QR code is even better. Customers scan the code with their phone camera and a chat opens immediately. This is ideal for physical locations like stores, restaurants, or event booths. Print the QR code on signage, receipts, or table tents.

Consider using message templates for common scenarios. A greeting message welcomes new customers. A follow-up reminder nudges customers who have not responded. An appointment scheduler lets customers book time slots. These templates save time and ensure consistent communication.

Always test your WhatsApp link before publishing it. Click the link on your own phone to confirm it opens the correct chat and the pre-filled message appears as expected. A broken link is worse than no link at all.
